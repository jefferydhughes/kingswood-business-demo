# Kingswood University website brand guide

Status: locked for the modernization build.

## Core palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep blue | `#061722` | Primary background, headings, navigation, body text |
| Kingswood red | `#972E2D` | Primary calls to action, emphasis, rules, active states |
| Grey | `#CCCCCC` | Secondary text on dark backgrounds, borders, focus indicators |
| Warm neutral | `#DED8D0` | Light section backgrounds and quiet surfaces |

White (`#FFFFFF`) is an accessibility utility for text and surfaces; it is not an additional accent colour.

## Colour rules

- Use only `#972E2D` for red interface elements. Pink, burgundy, coral, and brighter or darker red substitutions are not permitted.
- Deep blue is the dominant colour. Red is an accent and should not compete with the page hierarchy.
- Opacity may be applied to deep blue, grey, or white for overlays and borders. Do not create translucent red variants.
- Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Use white text on red for large buttons and deep blue text on warm-neutral or white surfaces.
- The supplied university logo is master artwork and must not be recoloured in CSS. New logo exports should use the locked red when Kingswood supplies an updated master.

## Logo

- Use the supplied Kingswood University wordmark from the repository assets.
- Keep its aspect ratio and clear space of at least half the flame-mark width.
- Do not stretch, crop, rotate, add shadows, or place it on visually busy imagery.
- On the website, place the light wordmark on deep blue.

## Photography

- Prefer candid, human images showing students doing meaningful work together.
- Choose real Kingswood photography whenever available. Stock imagery is temporary and must be tagged for replacement.
- Crop mobile-first around faces and activity; never place essential text inside an image.
- Use restrained saturation and a deep-blue overlay when text or proof points overlap photography.

## Interface direction

- Mobile-first, high-contrast, editorial, and direct.
- Primary enrollment actions remain visible without overwhelming program evidence.
- Use red for one primary action per decision area. Secondary actions use deep-blue, white, or outlined treatments.
- Avoid decorative gradients that introduce unapproved colours.
